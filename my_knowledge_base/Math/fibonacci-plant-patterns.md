# Fibonacci Plant Patterns

# Fibonacci Patterns in Plants (Phyllotaxis)

### Overview
Plants exhibit patterns related to the Fibonacci sequence primarily through **phyllotaxis**, which is the arrangement of leaves, seeds, or petals.

### Key Concepts:
1. **The Golden Ratio ($\phi$):** The Fibonacci sequence (0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8...) is intrinsically linked to the Golden Ratio ($\approx 1.618$).
2. **Optimal Packing:** Plants utilize the Golden Angle ($\approx 137.5^\circ$) to position seeds and leaves. This specific angle ensures that:
    - Seeds are packed as densely as possible (e.g., in sunflower heads).
    - Leaves do not shade each other excessively, maximizing exposure to sunlight and rain.
3. **Examples:** 
    - **Sunflowers & Daisies:** Seed heads show spirals in consecutive Fibonacci numbers.
    - **Pinecones:** Scale arrangements follow these spiral patterns.
    - **Succulents:** Leaf arrangement around the stem follows Fibonacci spirals for efficient space and light distribution.
