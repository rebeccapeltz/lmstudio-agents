# Pythagorean Theorem
The **Pythagorean theorem** is a fundamental principle in geometry that describes the relationship between the sides of a right-angled triangle.

### Formula:
$$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$

### Breakdown:
*   **$a$ and $b$**: The lengths of the two shorter sides (the "legs").
*   **$c$**: The length of the longest side (the **hypotenuse**), which is always opposite the right angle.

### Summary:
The sum of the squares of the two shorter sides equals the square of the hypotenuse. It is used to find a missing side length in a right-angled triangle.
