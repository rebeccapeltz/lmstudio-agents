# Glucose_Formula

# Glucose
The chemical formula for glucose is **C6H12O6**.

**Breakdown:**
*   **C6**: 6 Carbon atoms
*   **H12**: 12 Hydrogen atoms
*   **O6**: 6 Oxygen atoms

Glucose is a simple sugar (carbohydrate) that serves as a primary source of energy for living organisms.
